# Updating your website

Everything on emilykathryn.com that changes often, you can change yourself. No
one needs to touch the code, and nothing you do here can take the site down.

---

## Signing in

Go to **emilykathryn.com/studio** and sign in with the email address your
invitation was sent to.

Bookmark that address. It is the only one you need.

If you get an error saying you are not allowed, or the page just spins, tell
Jeff. That is a settings problem on our side, not something you did.

---

## The short version

1. Click the thing you want to change in the left-hand list.
2. Type.
3. Click **Publish** in the bottom right.

Your change is on the live website within a few seconds. Refresh the page to
see it.

**Nothing you type is live until you press Publish.** You can leave a page
half-finished, close the tab, and come back tomorrow. Your draft waits for you.

## Changing words on a page

Open the page, and use the tabs across the top to jump to the part you want.
The tabs are named after what a visitor sees: **Top of Page**, **The
Experience**, **Questions**, **Closing**.

Everything already has your current wording in it. You are editing what is
there, not starting from a blank page.

A few boxes have a small note under them explaining what they do. Those are
worth reading once.

---

## Adding or swapping photographs

Go to **My Galleries**.

- **Senior Portfolio** is the grid on the senior portraits page.
- **Family Portfolio** is the grid on the family portraits page.
- **Recent Work** is the grid on the front page.

Open one, then drag photos in, drag them out, or drag them into a different
order. Order in the list is the order on the page.

**Write a description for every photo you add.** The box is called Photo
Description. One plain sentence: "Senior boy in a letterman jacket beside a
truck." It is what a blind visitor's screen reader announces, and it is a real
part of how Google finds your photographs in image search. A photo with no
description is a photo Google cannot read.

If you would rather start a whole new gallery each season, you can. Create it,
set its type to Senior or Family, and it replaces the old grid. Your old
gallery stays in the list as an archive.

## Adding a review

**Client Testimonials** → the pencil icon at the top → fill in their name and
paste what they wrote.

Turn on **Show on Homepage?** for the ones you want on the front page. You can
feature as many as you like.

Paste reviews exactly as the client wrote them. Do not tidy them up. A
testimonial in someone else's words is worth something; a testimonial in yours
is not.

## The availability bar

**Availability Alerts** is the dark strip that says things like "Only 5 Spring
2027 senior slots left."

Write the message, turn on **Currently Showing?**, and publish. Turn it off
when it stops being true.

You can set an **Auto-Hide Date** and it will disappear on its own, which is
the easy way to avoid a bar advertising last spring's openings in October.

Keep only one turned on at a time.

## Questions and answers

The **Questions** tab on the Home, Senior and Family pages is not just text on
the page. Google and the AI assistants read those answers directly and quote
them when somebody asks a question about senior photos in your area.

So it is worth writing them properly:

- Ask the question the way a parent would actually ask it.
- Answer in full sentences, and put the answer first.
- Two to four sentences is about right. One line gets skipped.

Keep at least three questions on each page. Google ignores a list shorter than
that.

## Pricing

Your two packages are sitting in **Pricing & Packages** as **drafts**. They
have placeholder numbers in them.

Draft means they are visible to you and invisible to the website. Nothing is
showing a price to anyone right now.

When your real pricing is settled, correct the numbers and press Publish, and
they go live on both service pages. Talk to Jeff first, because your Investment
page is currently hidden and turning it back on is a separate switch.

## Things you cannot break

- **You cannot delete a page.** The delete option is turned off on all of them.
- **You cannot break a web address.** The parts that decide a page's URL are
  locked.
- **Clearing a box does not leave a hole.** Every part of the site has its
  current wording built into it as a backup. Empty a box and the page quietly
  goes back to what it says today.
- **Publish is undoable.** Every document keeps its history. Open the ⋮ menu at
  the top of a document and you can see and restore any earlier version.

## If something looks wrong

**"I published but the website looks the same."**
Give it about thirty seconds and do a hard refresh (Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac,
Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows). If it is still wrong after five minutes, tell Jeff.
There is a safety net that catches up within an hour regardless, so the change
is not lost.

**"I cannot sign in."**
Tell Jeff. Do not create a new Sanity account, it will not have access.

**"I deleted something I shouldn't have."**
Do not try to retype it. Tell Jeff. It is recoverable from the document's
history, and reconstructing it by hand is how the wrong version becomes
permanent.
