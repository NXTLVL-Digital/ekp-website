# Faith | 2019 Gretna High Senior

> If you want to see what a genuine, true beauty looks like...look no farther. Faith is a 2019 Gretna High senior and is one of the sweetest girls I've ever worked with. She reminds me SO much of my younger sister, Sarah, so I like her even more because of that! I've known her for years and photographed her older sister, Grace, several years ago when she was a senior. I was so happy when they contacted me this year for Faith's senior session. Faith has been doing dance for 15 years (starting at 3 years old!), so highlighting that during her session was so special. I always love photographing dancers.
