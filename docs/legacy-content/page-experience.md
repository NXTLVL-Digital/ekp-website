# experience

The Shoot!

Emily Kathryn Photography offers a custom photography experience, because we want your photos to be anything but generic! We want your session to reflect your style & personality, so the more we can customize your session, the better! EKP sessions are now all-inclusive -- so you get your professionally retouched digital images, as well as any prints/products that you may want. It's the best of both worlds.

session day

When you book your shoot, we chat about your vision for your session, so when your session day arrives, we have a game plan! We go over your outfit options and finalize locations. Want to channel more of an urban look? No problem, we have tons of great locations that fit that vibe perfectly. Are you more of a country girl who wants barns and gorgeous green pastures in her photos? We’ve got that covered too! Want a mixture of urban and rustic photos? You got it!

Our ultimate goal is that YOU love your photos, so we’re up for anything. We usually shoot for 2-3 hours, depending on which session option you choose, so be ready for a great time! The goal of the day is for you to have a fabulous, fun experience…so no stress is allowed! Don’t worry one bit about posing. That’s my job and I will guide you throughout the whole day. Oh...you better make plans to go out on the town after the shoot is over, because you’re going to look gorgeous!
