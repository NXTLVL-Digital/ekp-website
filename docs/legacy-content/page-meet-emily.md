# Emily

Hi there! I’m Emily…wife, mom and senior photographer! God has blessed me with an amazing husband, Jeff, and three beautiful kids, Ava, Owen and Elijah. They are our little joys; the greatest gifts God ever could have given us. And they keep us on our toes! Life is never boring at our house!

I grew up with a camera in my hands. I may not have had any clue what I was doing in the beginning, but I loved directing my sisters and cousins in “photo shoots” from a young age. It wasn’t until I was in college and started being asked by friends & family to take their photos that I realized that photography was more than just a hobby for me...it was what God put me on this earth to do. Nothing makes me happier than to take an absolutely stunning image of a senior girl and then to turn the camera around and show her just how beautiful she is! Instilling that sense of beauty and self-confidence is what my business is all about.

Senior year is such a big deal. You’ve worked so hard and now it’s your time. More than likely, the next time you’ll be photographed will be your wedding day, so that’s all the more reason to make your senior photography experience amazing. Take a look around my site and feel free to fill out the Contact form if you’d like to receive my Senior Magazine, which is packed full of information about my shoots, or to go ahead and set up a session date! I would love to be a part of your senior year!

I'd love to work with you!

Danville, Chatham, Gretna, Altavista, Evington, Virginia & Surrounding Areas
