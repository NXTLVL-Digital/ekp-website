# Raegan | 2019 Altavista Senior

> Meet Raegan, a 2019 Altavista High senior! I had the privilege of photographing her older sister, Alanna, a few years ago and was so excited to get to work with Raegan! She's a country girl, through and through, and wanted her style to be conveyed in her shoot... so we did just that! Of course she got glammed up with Rachael at Mint on Main (our makeup partner at our studio in Chatham, VA) with airbrush makeup and hairstyling and looked oh so gorgeous! What I love about working with senior girls is that we fit SO many different looks into their shoots! No limited outfit changes here! We want your style & personality to stand out. Raegan was able to do 7 different outfits and multiple locations in Chatham and Gretna. We started her session in town and then headed out to the country to shoot the rest of her adorable outfits. Enjoy a few of her faves from the session! She had so many good ones to choose from at her viewing session... Her hair & makeup...stunning! Then we stopped by my favorite tree-lined road for a few photos in this adorable dress! When I saw this American flag tank that Raegan brought to the shoot, I knew just where we had to shoot it! How cute is this?! One her of faves! Our last stop was our farm, which is filled with beautiful barns, fence-lines and green pastures...right up Raegan's alley! I knew when I shot these that her parents were going to love them and, sure enough, this was their canvas selection! It's going to look so great on their walls, right beside Alanna's senior portrait. Thanks for stopping by! Leave some love for Raegan and congratulate her on making it to her senior year!
