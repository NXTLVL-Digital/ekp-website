# Apple Business Connect Setup — Emily Kathryn Photography

Gets Emily Kathryn Photography onto Apple Maps, Siri, and Spotlight search —
where a large share of iPhone-carrying parents actually search. Free, ~30
minutes. Written so Emily can complete it without developer help.

> **Do this AFTER Google Business Profile setup** so the phone number and
> details are already decided and consistent everywhere.

---

## 1. Sign in

1. Go to [businessconnect.apple.com](https://businessconnect.apple.com).
2. Sign in with an Apple ID you want to own the listing long-term (your regular Apple ID is fine — you can add users later).
3. Choose **Small business** when asked about company size.

## 2. Add the business

1. Business name: **Emily Kathryn Photography** (exactly — no keyword stuffing).
2. Category: **Photographer** (primary). Add *Portrait Studio* as secondary if offered.
3. Address: Apple's flow is built around locations. Enter the Gretna address when asked.
   - If a **"My business doesn't have a storefront customers visit"** or service-area option is offered, choose it and hide the street address.
   - If the flow requires a visible address and you're not comfortable, pause and flag it to Jeff rather than publishing your home address — we would rather have no Apple listing than a public home address.
4. Service area: list the same seven communities as GBP where the form allows (Chatham, Danville, Lynchburg, Smith Mountain Lake, Forest, Altavista, Evington — all Virginia).

## 3. Verification

Apple verifies the business before the listing goes live — usually a phone
call/text to the business number, sometimes a request for a document
(business license, insurance, or utility bill matching the name/address).
Check the Business Connect dashboard for status; it can take a few days.

## 4. Complete the "place card" (what iPhone users see)

Fill in everything — complete cards rank and convert better:

- **Phone:** the same number, in the same format, as GBP and the website.
- **Website:** `https://emilykathryn.com`
- **Hours:** Mon–Sat 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (keep identical to GBP/site).
- **Description** — suggested draft:
  > Editorial senior portraits and family photography serving South-Central Virginia — Chatham, Danville, Lynchburg, Smith Mountain Lake, and surrounding communities. Magazine-style sessions for seniors and timeless family portraits.
- **Photos:** cover photo + 8–10 portfolio images (same set as GBP for consistency). Landscape-oriented images display best on place cards.
- **Action link (Showcase / "Book" button):** point to `https://emilykathryn.com/contact` with the label **Book a Session** (Apple offers preset actions — choose the reservation/quote style link).

## 5. After setup

- [ ] Search "Emily Kathryn Photography" in Apple Maps on an iPhone — confirm the card appears and the street address is not shown (or only the town shows).
- [ ] Tap the website and Book links on the card — both should open the site.
- [ ] Confirm name, phone, hours match GBP and the site exactly (NAP consistency).
- [ ] Revisit quarterly: refresh photos and confirm details — Apple occasionally "enhances" listings with data from other sources, so check nothing wrong crept in.

**Why this matters:** Apple Maps results feed Siri and Spotlight on every
iPhone. Most photographers never claim their listing — being complete here is
a free edge in exactly the demographic (iPhone families) that books portrait
sessions.
