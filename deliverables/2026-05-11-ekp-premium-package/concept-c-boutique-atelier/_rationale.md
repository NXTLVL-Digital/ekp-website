# Concept C — Boutique Atelier

The third of three concepts. Ultra-minimal, gallery-white, quiet-luxury direction — the most restrained option, designed for the buyer who recognizes that confidence often shows up as silence.

## Strategic Frame

Concept C is built for **Maren (the Lake-House Mom)** as a primary visual signal and for the most discerning version of Rachel — the buyer who has hired interior designers, who reads The Row's website to see how a brand should be presented, and who recoils from anything that feels like *trying*.

Design references: The Row, Khaite, Toteme, KT Merry, Norma Kamali atelier pages, Jose Villa minimal portfolio sites. The brand voice here speaks at a near-whisper. Every page lets the photography do the talking.

## Voice / Headline

> *A portrait studio for seniors and families.*

That's the whole homepage hero. Subhead: "Considered portraiture, made on location across the Danville–Lynchburg–Smith Mountain Lake corridor." The audacious move is the *restraint*. Most photographers can't resist over-explaining; this concept refuses to.

## Design System

| Element | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Cream (`#FCFAF5`) — slightly cooler than A, slightly warmer than B | Gallery-white feel |
| Display type | Italiana at very large sizes only | The single editorial signature |
| Body type | Inter at light weight (300) | Confident silence; readable but quiet |
| Italic emphasis | Cormorant Garamond italic | Reserved for leads and pull quotes only |
| Accent | Gold (`#B5996A`) as hairline rules only — never as a color block | Quiet luxury |
| Color | Strictly ink + cream + hairline gold. **No other colors.** | Discipline |
| Hero | Single image with massive whitespace above and below, two-column headline + sub | Atelier-page convention |
| Eyebrows | All-caps Inter at 0.66rem with 0.5–0.6em letter-spacing | Editorial restraint |
| Buttons | Outlined, large, all-caps, generous letter-spacing | Couture-house button language |
| Portfolio | Spacious grid with subtle grayscale-on-load → full color on hover | Gallery hanging convention |
| Section dividers | Centered hairline rules in gold, never bold rules | Quiet rhythm |

## Pages

Same 8-page structure:

1. **Home** — hero with single image and one-line headline; spare service overview; centered pull quote; airy portfolio grid; journal teaser; minimal booking CTA.
2. **About** — large image of Emily, italic-lead opening, atelier-style 5-step process with em-dashed numerals.
3. **Senior Portraits** — service hero with full-width image, atelier-split content/image alternation, dedicated boys section in cream.
4. **Family Portraits** — second-service hero, atelier rhythm, Smith Mountain Lake section *as a peer service*, not an afterthought.
5. **Investment** — table-style "Invest List" with four rows (Senior · Heirloom · Family · Lake-House) — Lake-House gets its own row, signaling the SML vertical seriously.
6. **Portfolio** — paginated by service line with centered eyebrow labels separating senior and family sections.
7. **Journal** — two-column journal with six posts, oversized type, gallery rhythm.
8. **Contact** — atelier-style form with underline-only inputs, italic display title "Begin."

## Where This Concept Wins

- Lands the SML / lake-house buyer better than either A or B
- Recognizes the buyer who is *over* trying-hard websites
- Hardest to copy — its excellence comes from discipline, not flourish
- Aligned with national luxury benchmarks (The Row, Khaite, Toteme as web references)
- Makes the photography the entire star
- Restraint reads as confidence at the upper-boutique price point

## Where This Concept Takes Risk

- May read as cold to the warmth-seeking parent buyer
- The Gen-Z senior (Ella) may find it "boring" on first scroll — needs the portfolio to do the persuading
- Less obvious "personality" than A or B — relies on the strength of the photography to carry it
- Phone presence may need to be louder than the website (Instagram should carry the human warmth)

## What Would Need to Be Truthful at Launch

- Photography quality must be at the level the design implies — no rescue from layout flourish
- Real portfolio with 30+ best-of images per service line
- A *real* lake-house family portfolio to validate the SML row in the investment list
- An actual investment guide PDF behind the "Request the Guide" CTA
- An Instagram presence that adds warmth the website intentionally leaves out

## The Three-Concept Strategic Read

| Concept | Best For | Strategic Bet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A — Editorial Heirloom** | The safe premium choice. Highest conversion among Rachel-buyers. | A polished version of the existing site that's parent-immediately credible. |
| **B — Modern Muse** | Where Ella sends the link. Highest senior-engagement potential. | Win the influencer first; let her bring her mom along. |
| **C — Boutique Atelier** | Where Maren feels at home. Unlocks the SML lake-house vertical. | Compete with national luxury photographers, not local ones. |

The studio could ship A and grow into C over 24 months. Or ship C and quietly let A's structural learnings inform iterations. B is more risk and more reward, depending on how confidently the studio leans into Gen-Z-first marketing.
