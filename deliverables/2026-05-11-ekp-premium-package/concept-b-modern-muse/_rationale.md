# Concept B — Modern Muse

The second of three concepts. Distinctly Gen-Z-forward, contemporary fashion-editorial in feel — designed to make Ella send the link to her friends.

## Strategic Frame

Concept B is built for **Ella (the Style-Aware Senior)** as the primary visual signal, with Rachel still trusting it underneath. The bet: if the senior actively wants this photographer, Rachel will read the credibility signals in the supporting copy and confirm.

Design references: SSENSE, Aritzia mood, contemporary fashion-editorial websites, Madewell era, Pinterest mood-board energy, scrap-book editorial. Less classical than Concept A, more *current.*

## Voice / Headline

> *Senior year is the cover. Let's shoot it like one.*

Lowercase styling throughout. Italic optical-sizing in the display serif as a design feature. Marquee text across the top of every page (animated, mint-on-black). Sticker labels with rotation. Slight image tilts. Aqua/mint as the primary accent color rather than gold.

## Design System

| Element | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Cream (`#FBF8F2`) — warmer than Concept A | Reads younger and friendlier |
| Display type | DM Serif Display with Fraunces italic optical sizing | Bigger personality, expressive italics |
| Body type | Inter | Same family system across concepts |
| Accent | Aqua/Mint (`#B3D4CD`) primary, gold reserved | Pulls forward the mint reference from the brand board |
| Buttons | Rounded pill, hover-lift, lowercase | Contemporary fashion-shop convention |
| Marquee | Animated mint band on every page | Editorial publication / scrolling-news convention |
| Stickers | Circular rotated labels on hero ("class of '27 open") | Sticker / collage editorial detail |
| Image tilts | Slight rotation (`-1deg` to `2deg`) on select tiles | Scrap-book / mood-board feel |
| Service panes | Two large image-overlay panes with editorial typography | Single-tap senior/family routing |
| Tab pills | Portfolio filter (visual, doesn't filter live) | Suggests content depth, looks modern |

## Pages

Same 8-page structure as Concept A; visual treatment is radically different:

1. **Home** — asymmetric hero with image grid + sticker, marquee, two-pane services, mint pull-quote, mood-board portfolio.
2. **About** — magazine-portrait of Emily, italic-heavy display type, italic-headed process steps.
3. **Senior Portraits** — split hero, "01" big-num column treatment, dedicated Senior Boys section with mint banner.
4. **Family Portraits** — different sticker color, big-num "02," Smith Mountain Lake section in full mint.
5. **Investment** — rounded-corner cards with "Most Popular" pill badge on the featured collection.
6. **Portfolio** — interactive tab-pill filters (visual, demonstrative), tilted image mosaic.
7. **Journal** — editorial journal cards with italicized headlines.
8. **Contact** — full inquiry form with rounded inputs and mint-accent focus states.

## Where This Concept Wins

- Makes the senior want to share the link immediately
- Distinctly different from every local competitor
- The most contemporary-feeling of the three options
- Maintains brand cohesion (palette, type system) without sacrificing differentiation
- Aqua accent pulls forward an underused brand board color

## Where This Concept Takes Risk

- Risks reading "too young" to the most conservative parent buyer
- Sticker / marquee / rotation moves are intentional and might polarize
- Italic-heavy display type takes confidence to commission and license correctly

## What Would Need to Be Truthful at Launch

- 18+ senior boy portraits to deliver the "boys + girls welcome" sticker honestly
- Working tab-pill filter behavior (currently demonstrative)
- Animated marquee may need to be more accessible (respect `prefers-reduced-motion`)
- Real journal posts to inhabit the editorial-magazine framing
- Smith Mountain Lake portfolio to validate the lake-house family vertical
