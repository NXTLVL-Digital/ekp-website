Three original directions plus a new fourth direction (04) built from Emily's reaction to round one — leaning into the calm of Direction 02 with subtle editorial flourishes from Direction 01. Click any card to open the full scrollable mockup.

Vol. 01 · Editorial
01

Editorial Magazine

Vogue · Bold · Fashion

Bold oversized serif headlines, asymmetric magazine-style layouts, numbered sections, and a marquee of brand language. Reads like the cover of a fashion publication — leans hardest into the "editorial/Vogue" brief.

Open Design 01

02

Modern Minimalist

Soft · Refined · Approachable

Generous whitespace, an off-white cream palette, delicate italic serifs, and a Pinterest-style masonry gallery. Calmer and more romantic — appeals strongly to parents and seniors of every gender without feeling overly "girly."

Open Design 02
Boutique · Vol. 03
03

Boutique Luxury

Moody · Cinematic · Premium

Black backgrounds, gold accents, full-bleed cinematic imagery and a sticky glass nav. Reads like a high-end fashion or hotel brand — most distinctive direction, and the strongest pull for senior boys and the "high-end" positioning.

04

Editorial Minimalist

Quiet · Considered · Magazine

The Minimalist's calm cream palette and breathable whitespace, with subtle editorial cues borrowed from 01: a "Vol. XII" running head, italic Roman-numeral sections, plate captions on imagery, and asymmetric magazine spreads — all without the heavy slabs or loud marquee.
