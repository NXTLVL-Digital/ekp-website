Hi, I'm Emily.

I'm a senior & family portrait photographer based in Gretna, Virginia. For more than ten years I've been making portraits I'd want to hang on my own wall — soft, slow, full of light, and always made for the wonderful person in front of the camera.

The Experience

What it's like.

i.

Inquire

Send a quick note about your senior, your family, or the look you've been dreaming up. I'll send back a full pricing & experience guide.

ii.

Plan

We'll meet (in person or over Zoom) to talk wardrobe, locations, and the vision for your session — every detail is intentional.

iii.

Shoot

Your session day — playlists, snacks, and a relaxed pace built around getting the kind of frames you'll actually want to print.

Reveal

An in-person reveal & ordering session where you'll see your final gallery and choose the heirlooms for your home.
