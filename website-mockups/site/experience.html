— The EKP Experience —

A custom experience.

Because your photos should be anything but generic — and your senior year is anything but ordinary.

Emily Kathryn Photography offers a custom photography experience because we want your photos to be anything but generic.

We want your session to reflect your style and personality — so the more we can customize, the better. EKP sessions are now all-inclusive, which means you get your professionally retouched digital images and any prints or products you may want. The best of both worlds.

When you book your shoot, we chat about your vision so when your session day arrives, we have a game plan. We go over outfit options and finalize locations. Want a more urban look? No problem — we have tons of locations that fit that vibe. More of a country girl who wants barns and gorgeous green pastures? We've got that covered too. Want a mixture of urban and rustic? You got it.

Our ultimate goal is that you love your photos, so we're up for anything. Sessions usually run 2–3 hours depending on the option you choose, so be ready for a great time. The day is built to be fabulous and fun — no stress allowed. Don't worry one bit about posing. That's my job, and I'll guide you the whole way.

Oh — and you better make plans to go out on the town after the shoot. You're going to look gorgeous.

From Inquiry to Reveal

Four unhurried steps

i.

Inquire

Send a quick note about your senior or your family. I'll reply with the Senior Magazine — packed with details, pricing, and sample images.

ii.

Plan

We chat — in person or by phone — to talk vision, wardrobe, and locations. Every detail is intentional and tailored to you.

iii.

Shoot

Your session day. 2–3 unhurried hours. Playlists, snacks, and a relaxed pace built around getting frames you'll love and want to print large.

iv.

Reveal

An in-person reveal & ordering session two weeks after your shoot — see all your retouched images and choose the heirlooms for your home.

— The Final Product —

The truly exciting part.

Seeing all of your gorgeous images for the first time is the truly exciting part. I'll post sneak peeks on Instagram and Facebook, and your professionally retouched digital images will be ready two weeks after your shoot. Your session also includes a Model App for your phone — so showing off your images on social is easy.

We strongly believe your images aren't meant to live on your computer forever. EKP has been providing clients with luxe, heirloom-quality products for twelve years now, and nothing brings us more joy than seeing your photos in print. We offer in-person ordering sessions to help you pick out the products that are best for you — whether that's a pearlescent leather album, a fine art canvas, or a wall gallery for your home.

You're going to love the final results.
