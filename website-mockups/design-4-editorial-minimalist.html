— Behind The Camera —

Hi, I'm Emily.

I'm a senior & family portrait photographer based in Gretna, Virginia. For more than a decade I've been making portraits I'd want to hang on my own wall — soft, slow, full of light, and made entirely for the wonderful person in front of the camera.

Whether you're a senior dreaming up your dream session, a parent wanting to mark a milestone, or a family ready to slow down for an afternoon — I'd love to make something beautiful with you.

The experience

— four unhurried steps —
i.

Inquire

Send a quick note about your senior, family, or the look you've been imagining. I'll reply with a full pricing & experience guide.

ii.

Plan

We meet — in person or over Zoom — to talk wardrobe, locations, and the vision for your session. Every detail is intentional.

iii.

Shoot

Reveal

An in-person reveal & ordering session where you'll see your final gallery and choose the heirlooms for your home.
