       - Altavista: Focus on the Lane family legacy, the small-town heart, the Staunton River connection, the community pride. Talk about how this town punches above its weight.
       - Evington: Focus on the rural tranquility, the Bedford County beauty, the rolling countryside, families who value tradition. Talk about capturing life's milestones surrounded by Virginia's natural beauty.

       - Written in Emily's brand voice: warm, confident, editorial (not salesy, not template-y)
